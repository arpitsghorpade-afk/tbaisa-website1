Company Overview

Buyer-Focused Export Support — Not Generic Trading

TBAISA Import Export is an India-based Import & Export business focused on spices, agro commodities, food commodities and fresh produce for international B2B buyers. We operate as a sourcing partner — aligning product, packing and paperwork to your buying requirement rather than pushing fixed catalogs.

Every inquiry is handled with attention to specifications, destination requirements and commercial volumes. Relevant export documentation can be provided according to product and destination.
